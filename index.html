On privacy

We're building during a moment when everything runs on training data. The standard playbook: get users, study users, monetize users. AI has made this faster and more thorough than ever. Our apps don't participate. Data stays on your device. No accounts. Nothing leaves unless you send it yourself.

On pricing

Subscriptions made sense when software had servers to run. Most utility apps don't — yet here we are, paying $8/month for a to-do list. Basic features are free forever. The full version is a one-time purchase. No renewal dates to dread, no tiers that nudge you upward, no price increases after you forgot you subscribed.
