Privacy

Last updated — May 2026

Your data stays on your device. We don't run servers that receive it — so there's nothing to breach, sell, or subpoena. All three apps work this way.

In practice: no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporters, no third-party SDKs phoning home. Purchases go through Apple directly. We never see your payment details.

macOS

ShowPoint

ShowPoint watches your mouse position and clicks using macOS's standard event monitoring API — that's how it knows where to draw the halo and when to fire a pulse. That data is processed and discarded in real time. It never leaves your Mac.

Your license state (whether you've bought Lifetime Unlock or used the 10-minute trial) is stored in your Mac's Keychain, Apple's built-in encrypted credential store. That's local only. We have no servers to sync it to.

  • No screen recording permission — ShowPoint is a transparent overlay, not a recorder
  • Mouse position and click events are read-only and discarded immediately after rendering
  • License state in Keychain — on your Mac, stays on your Mac
  • StoreKit purchases handled entirely by Apple
iOS

LumaSip

Your intake log lives in the app on your phone. If you turn on HealthKit sync, that goes to Apple Health on-device — we don't read from Apple Health ourselves, and nothing syncs to our servers. There aren't any.

  • Intake data stored locally on device
  • HealthKit read/write is on-device only — we never access your Health data
  • No account required
iOS — coming soon

Period Tracker

Cycle data stays in the app's local database. It doesn't leave your device. If you back up your phone to iCloud, that backup is between you and Apple — we're not involved.

  • Cycle and symptom data stored locally — never transmitted
  • No account, no sync to our end
  • iCloud device backup is Apple's infrastructure, not ours
