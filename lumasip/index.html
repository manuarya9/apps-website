LumaSip

A water tracker that doesn't need to know who you are. Log intake, set reminders, sync with Apple Health — everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no funny business.

iOS 17+ Free HealthKit Widgets
Download on App Store

Features

  • Quick-add logging — tap once to log a cup, or enter a custom amount in ml or oz
  • Smart reminders — quiet hours, auto-skip when you've already hit your goal, adjustable cadence
  • Apple Health sync — dietary water samples written and read, no double-counting
  • Home and lock screen widgets — today's progress without opening the app
  • Streak tracking — see how many days in a row you've hit your goal
  • History view — edit or delete past entries, see trends over time
  • No account, no cloud, no tracking — data lives on your device, full stop

Privacy

LumaSip stores all data locally using SwiftData. No account required. No network analytics. Health data is only accessed with your permission and is never sent anywhere — not to us, not to anyone.
