LumaSip

Track your daily water intake without signing up for anything. Smart reminders, Apple Health sync, widgets, and streaks — all stored on your device.

iOS 17+ Free HealthKit Widgets
Download on App Store

Features

  • Quick-add logging — tap once to log a cup, or enter a custom amount in ml or oz
  • Smart reminders — quiet hours, smart skip when you've already hit your goal, adjustable cadence
  • Apple Health sync — dietary water samples written and read, no double-counting
  • Home and lock screen widgets — see today's progress at a glance
  • Streak tracking — stay motivated with a running streak of days you hit your goal
  • History view — edit or delete past entries, see trends over time
  • No account, no cloud, no tracking — everything stays on your iPhone

Privacy

LumaSip stores all data locally using SwiftData. No account required. No network analytics. Health data is only accessed with your explicit permission and is never sent anywhere.
