Common questions

How do I hide my desktop icons on a Mac before screen sharing?

Press one hotkey. Cloak hides every desktop icon instantly and brings them all back when you press it again — no Terminal commands, no dragging files into folders.

Can Cloak silence notifications during a screen share?

Yes. Cloak turns on Do Not Disturb via a bundled macOS Shortcut you install once — Slack messages and calendar alerts stay quiet for the entire presentation.

Does Cloak activate automatically when I join a Zoom or Teams call?

With the Lifetime Unlock, Cloak watches for Zoom, Teams, Meet, Loom, or OBS coming to the foreground and fires your chosen profile automatically — then restores everything the moment the app closes.

What happens if Cloak crashes while my desktop is hidden?

Cloak snapshots what it hid the moment it activates. On relaunch it finds and restores everything, so a force quit never leaves you with a permanently hidden desktop.

Is it a subscription?

No. The basics are free forever, and the Lifetime Unlock is a one-time $6.99 purchase. No accounts, no renewals.
