Cloak

Share your screen without the pre-share scramble. One keystroke hides desktop icons, silences notifications, and clears the wallpaper. Pro adds window cover and auto-triggers so it's already done before you hit "Share Screen."

macOS Free tier $6.99 lifetime Menu Bar
Download on Mac App Store

What it does

  • One-keystroke mode — hides desktop icons, silences notifications, and clears the wallpaper. Nothing leaks.
  • Auto-trigger (Pro) — fires when Zoom, Teams, Meet, Loom, or OBS comes to the foreground; restores when it closes
  • Window cover (Pro) — hides your own app windows behind a clean surface. Restores completely when you're done.
  • Per-app profiles (Pro) — Meeting and Recording modes with separate behaviors for each
  • Crash-safe restore — snapshots what was hidden on activation. A force quit won't leave you stuck.
  • Menu-bar only — no Dock icon, no window in the way
