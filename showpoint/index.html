What it does

  • Cursor halo — a persistent ring around your pointer so viewers always know where you are
  • Click pulses — distinct left and right click animations that flash on every click
  • Spotlight mode — dims the rest of the screen to focus attention on your cursor area
  • Modifier-key HUD — shows ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ as you hold them, so shortcuts are always visible
  • Click-only mode — show the overlay only when you click, not constantly
  • Multi-monitor — works seamlessly across all connected displays
  • Per-app presets — different settings for Zoom, Loom, Xcode, and more
