What it does

  • Cursor halo — a persistent ring around your pointer so viewers always know where you are
  • Click pulses — distinct left and right click animations that flash on every click
  • Spotlight mode — dims the rest of the screen so attention follows your cursor
  • Modifier-key HUD — shows ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ as you hold them, so your shortcuts are actually legible
  • Click-only mode — hide the halo until you click, for a cleaner presentation
  • Multi-monitor — works across all connected displays
  • Per-app presets — different settings for Zoom, Loom, Xcode, whatever you're in
