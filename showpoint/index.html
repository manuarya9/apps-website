ShowPoint

Your cursor is somewhere on screen. Your viewers just can't find it. ShowPoint adds a halo, click pulses, and spotlight on top of any recorder you already use. Free basics cover meetings and live demos; Lifetime Unlock adds the Tutorial preset, modifier-key HUD, presenter effects, Pro styles, and extra hotkeys.

What it does

  • Cursor halo — a persistent ring around your pointer so viewers always know where you are
  • Click pulses — distinct left and right click animations that flash on every click
  • Spotlight mode — dims the rest of the screen so attention follows your cursor
  • Modifier-key HUD — available in the Pro trial or Lifetime Unlock, showing ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ ⇧ state without showing typed keys
  • Click-only mode — a Pro workflow for cleaner recordings when a persistent halo feels noisy
  • Multi-monitor — works across all connected displays
  • Presets — Live Demo and Meeting are free; Tutorial is built for teaching and live coding
