Why it's not the VW badge

  • VW stacks two letters (V over W) — Vigilant is a single V read as a face, with eyes.
  • VW's strokes fill the circle edge-to-edge — Vigilant's V floats inset, leaving an open field.
  • The eyes are the dominant cue: the brain reads "watcher," not "monogram."
  • The open-aperture variant breaks the ring entirely — a structural departure VW can't share.
